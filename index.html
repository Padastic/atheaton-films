Φιλία εστί µία ψυχή εν δυσί σώµασιν ενοικουµένη

From a temporary project to a lifelong craft.

If you had told me 20 years ago that I’d dedicate my life to wedding filmmaking, I'd think you are out of your mind!

I began in 2005, working in television and commercial production. Weddings entered my life almost by accident. A temporary solution between film projects. That changed in 2009, during a Robert Balasko seminar in Athens. I realized then that a wedding isn’t simple event coverage. It’s raw, cinematic storytelling.

That moment changed everything. I realized that the most powerful stories don’t happen on sets, they unfold naturally, in front of us. Atheaton Films was born from the desire to bring cinematic direction into real human moments.

Over the years, I’ve traveled across Greece, drawn by places and people. Connection is what fuels my work. Every wedding is a new story, a new emotional journey.

My goal is simple: years from now, when you watch your film, I want you to feel exactly what you felt that day. To remember how it looked and how it felt to be there.

"From the need to communicate, humans created art. Language is one of its forms. Videography is the art of memory preservation. Sound and image working together to awaken emotion. That’s why rewatching your wedding film feels like traveling in time; back to the day itself."
